The Job Board — how an unmatched job finds its provider

When a customer's request goes unanswered by push offers, it stops dead-ending. It becomes searchable: nearby providers pull it, raise a hand with a price, and the customer chooses.

How a job leaves the board

  • Matched — a customer picked and the provider confirmed. Gone for everyone else.
  • Shortlist full — 3 hands are up; new providers see “this job just filled up”.
  • Past due — the customer's time window passes → hidden immediately.
  • Expired — 7-day lifetime ends → job closes, customer told, and every interested provider gets a courteous “no longer available, more jobs on your board”.
  • No-show pick — chosen provider never confirms → job quietly returns to the board.

What a browsing provider sees

VISIBLE BEFORE SELECTION

  • Trade + job title & description
  • Suburb only (e.g. Wilgeheuwel)
  • Requested time window
  • Interest count (n/3)

HIDDEN UNTIL MATCHED

  • Customer name
  • Phone number
  • Street address & gate codes
  • Any contact channel

Identity unlocks only after the customer chooses and the provider passes KYC + credit checks — the same gate as push leads. Off-platform poaching has nothing to work with.
